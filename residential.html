What we do in homes

Four things, done right

Repairs & diagnostics

The system stops cooling, stops heating, trips the breaker or runs and never gets there. First job is finding out why, and saying so plainly.

  • Not cooling, not heating, short cycling
  • Capacitors, contactors, motors, compressors
  • Refrigerant leaks and charge faults
  • Thermostats and controls
  • Blocked and overflowing condensate drains

System replacement

When a repair will not hold, the whole system gets replaced — condenser, air handler or furnace, plenums, line set and thermostat, sized to the house.

  • Condenser and air handler changeouts
  • Gas furnace replacement
  • New plenums and transitions
  • Thermostat set up and walked through

Ductwork

Rooms that never get comfortable are usually a duct problem, not an equipment problem. Runs get checked, repaired, resized or replaced.

  • Leaking, crushed or disconnected runs
  • Exhaust and vent duct repairs
  • Undersized returns and supply
  • New duct for additions and remodels

Mini-splits & add-ons

For garages, additions, sunrooms, shops and any room the main system was never sized to reach. Outdoor unit, line set and indoor head.

  • Ductless mini-split installation
  • Garage and workshop cooling
  • Additions the main system cannot cover
  • Line-set routing kept tidy

Questions

Common ones

My AC is running but the house is not cooling

That is usually a charge problem, a failing capacitor or compressor, a dirty coil or a duct fault letting the cold air go somewhere it should not. It needs diagnosing on site rather than guessed at over the phone.

Should I repair it or replace it?

Depends on the age of the system, what has failed and what else is close to failing. If a repair will genuinely get you through, that is what gets recommended. If it will not, you are told before money is spent on it.

There is water around the indoor unit

Almost always a blocked condensate drain. Switch the system off at the thermostat so it stops producing more water, and call. Leaving it running can put water through a ceiling.

One room is always hot or always cold

Usually ductwork — a crushed, leaking or disconnected run, or a return that is too small. Sometimes the room was never on the original design at all, in which case a mini-split is the fix.

Do you work outside normal hours?

Hours are 8am to 5pm. Outside them, extreme temperatures come first — no heat in winter, no cooling in summer. Call and say what the situation is.

What do you need from me when I call?

Your address, what the system is doing or not doing, and roughly how old it is. The brand helps if you know it.

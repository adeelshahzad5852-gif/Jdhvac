Commercial HVAC

Rooftop units, ductwork, equipment rooms and new construction across Spring, Tomball, The Woodlands and North Houston. Owner on site, not a crew you have never met.

What we take on

Four kinds of commercial job

Rooftop package units

Service, repair and full changeouts on rooftop units, including the lift when a unit has to come off or go onto a roof.

  • Diagnostics and repair on live units
  • Like-for-like and upgrade changeouts
  • Crane lifts arranged where a unit needs one

Ductwork

Exposed spiral for retail and showroom spaces, rectangular trunk and branch for warehouse and office. Fabricated and hung to suit the building.

  • Exposed spiral duct and diffusers
  • Rectangular trunk, plenums, transitions
  • Flexible duct drops and grilles
  • Replacement of failed or undersized runs

Equipment and server rooms

Cooling for rooms that cannot be allowed to get hot — telecom racks, battery plant, network and server equipment. Work done around powered, running equipment.

  • Dedicated air handlers for equipment rooms
  • Condensate routing away from racks
  • Working around powered equipment

New construction & build-out

New shells and build-outs — equipment set, duct run, air handlers hung and closets framed.

  • Equipment set on pads and roofs
  • Ceiling-hung and closet air handlers
  • Coordination with other trades on site

Our services

What we do

Air conditioning and heating, residential and light commercial, across North Houston.

AC repair

Not cooling, not turning on, tripping the breaker, short cycling or leaking water. Diagnosed on site, explained before anything is replaced.

Heating repair

Gas furnaces and heat pumps that will not fire, will not keep up, or shut down on safety. Checked, repaired and tested before winter.

System replacement

Complete changeouts — condenser, air handler or furnace, plenums, line set and thermostat, sized to the building.

Ductwork

Leaking, crushed or undersized runs, exhaust and vent repairs, and new duct for additions, remodels and commercial fit-outs.

Commercial rooftop units

Service, repair and full changeouts on RTUs for retail, warehouse and office buildings, including the lift when a unit has to come off a roof.

Equipment & server rooms

Cooling for rooms that cannot be allowed to get hot — telecom racks, battery plant, network and server equipment.

Questions

Before you call

Do you work on commercial systems?

Yes. Rooftop package units, spiral and rectangular ductwork, new construction build-outs and equipment-room cooling, alongside the residential side. See the commercial work.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Outside those hours, extreme temperatures come first — no heat in winter, no cooling in summer. Call and say what the situation is.

Can I text instead of calling?

Yes. Text (281) 706-8336 with your address and what the system is doing. If it is urgent, phone — that gets seen faster.

What should I have ready?

Your address, what the system is doing or not doing, and roughly how old it is. If you know the brand, say so. That is usually enough to know what to bring.

Do you replace whole systems, or only repair?

Both. Repairs, part replacements and complete changeouts. If a repair will get you through, that is what gets recommended.

Who will be doing the work?

JD HVACR Solutions is owner-operated, so the person who quotes the job is the person who does it. There is no call center and no dispatcher between you and the work.

System down? Call.

The phone gets answered first. Texts and form messages are picked up through the day.

Questions

Before you call

Who actually turns up?

Daniel. JD HVACR Solutions is owner-operated, so the person who quotes the job is the person who does it. There is no call center and no dispatcher between you and the work.

Do you work on commercial systems?

Yes. Rooftop package units, spiral and rectangular ductwork, new construction fit-outs and equipment-room cooling, alongside the residential side. See the commercial work.

What are your hours?

8am to 5pm. Outside those hours, extreme temperatures come first — no heat in winter, no cooling in summer. Call and say what the situation is.

Can I text instead of calling?

Yes. Text (281) 706-8336 with your address and what the system is doing. If it is an emergency, phone — it gets seen faster.

What should I have ready?

Your address, what the system is doing or not doing, and roughly how old it is. If you know the brand, say so. That is usually enough to know what to bring.

Do you replace whole systems, or only repair?

Both. Repairs, part replacements and complete changeouts — condenser, air handler, furnace, plenums and thermostat. If a repair will get you through, that is what gets recommended.

System down? Call.

Phone lines get answered first. Texts and form messages are picked up through the day.
